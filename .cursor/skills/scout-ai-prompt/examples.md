## Anti-pattern checklist (for system prompt)

- «неразрушающий» / «УЗК» in **equipment purchase** title → L3
- Обучение / аттестация / поверка / калибровка as main subject → L3
- Street/address «Энергетиков» ≠ energy sector object
- Do not use **класс опасности** or lab допуски in tier decision
- Railway / Transsib industrial NK service → usually L2 (soft), not hard ban
- AI may promote to L1 when rules wrongly kept L3 — expected (independent layer)
